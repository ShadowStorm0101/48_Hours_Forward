# 48 Hours Forward
### Group 48 2033 code submission


This application is designed to fulfil the United Nations goal of promoting good health and wellbeing.
Our application specifically aids users in quitting common addictions. 

Through our application users have access to finding help both through resources online, or through in person meetings through our map.

We encourage our users to stick with their goals, and we achieve this by giving them live feedback on how far they have come.
